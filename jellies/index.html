Jellies on the Run

A quick, cute sliding puzzle

Français

The sun is out and the jellies are melting!

Slide each jelly to the gate of its color and get them all out before the heat wins. One finger, ten seconds to learn, and trickier than it looks.

Coming soon on theApp Store

Free on iPhone. Android version planned.

How it works

  • Drag a jelly: it slides along the box until something blocks it.
  • Each jelly leaves through the gate of its color, if the gate is wide enough.
  • Jellies block each other, so the order matters.
  • Get everyone out before they melt!

What you get

  • Hundreds of levels that get trickier and trickier.
  • Short levels made for a quick break.
  • Three stars to earn on every level, and best times to beat.
  • Free to play; an optional purchase removes the ads between levels.
