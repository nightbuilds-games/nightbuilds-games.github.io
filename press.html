The studio

nightbuilds is an indie game studio founded in 2026 by Pierre Trochet, a solo developer based in France. It makes small, colorful puzzle games for phones: short sessions, playable with one hand, understood in ten seconds.

Jellies on the Run is its first game. Two more are in the making: Jewel Box and Lil’ Gobbler.

Jellies on the Run

Slide, sort, beat the heat!
The sun is out and the jellies are melting! Slide each one to the gate of its color before it’s too late. Quick, cute, and trickier than it looks.

The game in short

  • Drag a jelly and it slides until something blocks it. Each one leaves through the gate of its color, and they get in each other’s way: the order matters.
  • The sun is the opponent: jellies in the sun heat up and melt, those in the shade last longer.
  • Levels under a minute, three stars to earn, best times to beat.
  • Six worlds of twenty levels, each with its own scenery, character and music, then endless levels.
  • Something new every few levels: odd-shaped boxes, jellies stuck in ice, two-layer jellies, mystery crates, and three boosters (ice cubes, spoon, ladle).
  • Color-blind symbols, haptics, playable with one hand.
  • Hand-built without an off-the-shelf game engine: the game and its engine are written in JavaScript.

Jewel Box

Sort gems, fill every box
Every level is a jewelled object held together by gems. Tap them out in the right order, fill each box with three of a color, and the object comes apart piece by piece. A calm puzzle with no timer.

Developernightbuilds (Pierre Trochet), France
GenrePuzzle (sorting, take-apart)
Release dateTo be announced
PlatformsiPhone (iOS 15 or later). Android version planned.
PriceFree, with ads, no banners. Optional purchases: remove ads ($3.99), coin packs ($1.99 and $4.99).
LanguagesEnglish, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil)

Game pagenightbuilds.app/jewelbox · playable demo in the browser

Jewel Box in short

  • Tap a gem and it flies to the box of its color, or waits on a small tray if no such box is open. Three of a kind close the box.
  • Pieces overlap: a piece with no gems left drops away and reveals what it was hiding. The order matters, and a full tray ends the run.
  • 300 different objects to take apart, one per level: jewels, animals, treats, instruments, monuments.
  • Fifteen worlds of twenty levels, each with its own jewelled scenery and music, and a big, tricky object to close it.
  • New twists along the way: mystery gems, padlocks with a key to find, chained gems, and three boosters (extra slot, spare tray, hammer).
  • No timer, three stars to earn, color-blind symbols, playable with one hand.
  • Hand-built like Jellies on the Run: the game, its engine and the solver that guarantees every level are written in JavaScript.
