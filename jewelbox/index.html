Jewel Box

Every level is a jewelled object held together by gems. Tap them out in the right order, fill each box with three of a color, and watch the piece come apart.

Free300 objectsFifteen worldsNo timerPlays offline

One finger, no timerTen seconds to learn. Three stars is another story.

1

Tap

Tap a gem: it flies to the box of its color. No box yet? It waits on the tray.

2

Sort

Three of a kind close the box, and the next one comes in. You can see the next two: plan ahead.

3

Take apart

A piece with no gems left drops away and reveals what it was hiding. Empty the whole object without filling the tray.
