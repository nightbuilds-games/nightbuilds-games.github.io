One rule, pure logicA minute to learn. The big grids are another story.

1

Cross out

Tap a cell once to cross it out: no queen can go there. Drag to cross out several.

2

Place

Tap again to place a queen. Her row, her column, her zone and the cells around her are crossed out for you.

3

Deduce

A zone that fits in a single row, a column with one free cell left… Every grid falls to reasoning, never to luck.
